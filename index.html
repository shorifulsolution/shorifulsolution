About

I am an agricultural engineer and machine learning researcher with an MS in Farm Structure and Environmental Engineering from Bangladesh Agricultural University. My research combines experimental engineering, data analysis, and machine learning to develop practical, sustainable solutions for engineering and agricultural problems.

My interests span machine learning for engineering materials, neural networks, predictive modeling, sustainable and fiber-reinforced concrete, soil stabilization, irrigation and water management, and waste-material utilization. Across six published papers, I've worked on machine learning applications, sustainable construction materials, agricultural soils, irrigation, and groundwater recharge.

My research experience covers concrete and material testing, experimental design, statistical analysis, machine learning model development, and manuscript preparation, using MATLAB, Python, R, and SPSS. I'm currently working on fiber-reinforced concrete, sustainable construction materials, and data-driven concrete mix design, while continuing to explore ML applications across engineering and agricultural systems.

I'm looking for PhD opportunities and research collaborations internationally, particularly in interdisciplinary areas connecting civil, materials, and structural engineering with agricultural engineering, water resources, and machine learning.

Current work

Fiber-reinforced concrete paving blocks from lathe-waste steel scrap

BAURES Project No. 2025/28/BAU · funded

Developing paving blocks reinforced with steel fibers recovered from lathe-waste scrap, aimed at sustainable use in walkway construction — pairing experimental testing with an ANN-based mix design.

Data-driven prediction of concrete mix proportions

Machine learning framework

Building a machine learning framework to predict optimal concrete mix proportions from material and performance data, reducing reliance on trial-and-error mix design.

Compressive strength prediction with artificial neural networks

FRC with lathe-waste fiber

Modeling the compressive strength of fiber-reinforced concrete containing lathe-waste fiber, comparing ANN performance against conventional strength-prediction approaches.
